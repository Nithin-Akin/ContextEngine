Northstar Cloud Company Policy

Refund Policy:
Customers on monthly plans can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase.
Enterprise customers have a 60-day refund window.
Enterprise refunds are calculated on a pro-rated basis based on remaining contract time.

Password Security:
Passwords must contain at least 12 characters.
Users are required to enable two-factor authentication for administrator accounts.
Password reset links expire after 20 minutes.

Leave Policy:
Full-time employees receive 24 paid leave days per year.
Unused paid leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days.
Employees must request planned leave at least 3 working days in advance.

Support:
Standard customers receive email support during business hours.
Enterprise customers receive 24/7 priority support.
The target response time for enterprise critical incidents is 30 minutes.
